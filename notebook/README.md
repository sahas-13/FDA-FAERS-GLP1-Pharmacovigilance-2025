##  Background

GLP-1 receptor agonists have emerged as the most prescribed drug class globally 
in 2025, used for Type 2 Diabetes and obesity management. Despite their 
widespread use, post-market safety surveillance remains critical.

This project applies standard pharmacovigilance methodology to the FDA Adverse 
Event Reporting System (FAERS) to detect disproportionate safety signals for:

- **Semaglutide** — OZEMPIC, WEGOVY, RYBELSUS (Novo Nordisk)
- **Tirzepatide** — MOUNJARO, ZEPBOUND (Eli Lilly)

---

##  Objectives

1. Identify ADEs disproportionately associated with GLP-1 drugs vs all other drugs
2. Compare the safety profiles of Semaglutide vs Tirzepatide
3. Quantify outcome severity (death, hospitalization, disability)
4. Characterize the demographic profile of affected patients
5. Track safety signal trends across all 4 quarters of 2025
